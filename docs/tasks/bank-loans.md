# Bank and informal loans

A bank loan's plan is computed from its terms, with rate periods, extra payments, late instalments and a state subsidy that pays interest first. Every instalment counts as paid on its due date unless a delay is entered. Future payments at a variable rate show as estimates. An informal loan keeps movements and a balance.
