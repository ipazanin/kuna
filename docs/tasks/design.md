# Design wave

Kuna opens on a home with one card per app, and inside an app only its own sidebar shows. The design library lives in `src/shared/ui` with a gallery at `#/gallery`, ESLint and Prettier run in CI, and the visual check catches uneven form rows and grids that keep one arrangement at every width. The Loans Overview and loan screens were redesigned on top of it.
