# Open points in investment tax

The Tax screen marks each rule settled, a reading or unconfirmed. These wait on a source.

- Whether gains through a foreign broker take recipient code 1001 or 1003 on the JOPPD. Kuna writes 1001, marked unconfirmed.
- Whether tax-free gains still need a JOPPD in a year whose lots under two years end in a loss.
- The US treaty's remaining steps. The Croatian ratifying law is out, the treaty is not in force yet, and withholding changes from 1 January 2027 at the earliest.
- The 2023 surtax in JOPPD field 16.2. Kuna holds no 2023 surtax rates and shows 2023 before surtax.
- The Porezna register's 30-day column.
- Whether a d.o.o. may pay an advance on this year's profit, which the interim option on Company's Year, Pay tab assumes.

Each point moves into its law file once settled, then into the code.
