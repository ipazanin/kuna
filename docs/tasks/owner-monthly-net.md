# Owner monthly net

A tile beside Monthly net pay adds the period's profit still in the company, as if paid out as a dividend after corporate tax and dividend tax, to what the owner took home. The Owner time filter sets the period, and open owner loans are settled by the payout first. Rent paid to the owner counts after the owner's rent tax.
