# Co-owned assets and loans

Assets v1 counts an asset's full value, and Loans counts a loan in full. If a flat is shared, both figures are too high by the other owner's part. Loans' `yourShare` is the part of an instalment paid after a subsidy, not a share of ownership.

The v1 model is left easy to extend with an ownership share on an asset and on a loan.
