# Matching company and personal money

Salary, dividends and reimbursements leave the company ledger and land in the personal one. Matching them would show each payment once on both sides, and could start an owner advance from a personal row.

It needs spending's ledger and the published company facts. Each app keeps its own file and no write touches two, so a match is either recorded on one side or worked out on read.

## Open

Everything. It follows [spending v1](spending-v1.md) and [company facts](company-facts.md).
