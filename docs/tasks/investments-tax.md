# Investments tax

A dollar gain is worked out in dollars and converted once, at the HNB rate for the settlement day. The screen shows the year, sales, foreign income and what turns tax-free, and downloads the register. Each rule names the law file it rests on, and a spec keeps the two in step. What is still unsettled is in [open points in investment tax](investment-tax-open-points.md).
