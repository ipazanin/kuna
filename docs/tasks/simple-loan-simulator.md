# Simpler loan simulator

New scenario starts from four fields: amount, rate, term in years and months, and repayment type. Everything else waits in a closed section whose defaults change nothing. Scenarios compares new loans and offers only, led by the monthly instalment, and tries on an existing loan are saved on that loan's page. Equal principal repayment runs through the whole Loans engine.
