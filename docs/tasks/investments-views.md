# Investments views

Holdings shows its mix by sector, currency, country, class and account, with targets and closed positions. Performance gained a monthly heatmap, drivers, an underwater chart and a gain bridge. Income gained a forecast, a calendar and tax withheld by country. Trades show as dots on the price chart, and any ticker can be compared.
