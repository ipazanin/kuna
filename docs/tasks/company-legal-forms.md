# More Croatian legal forms

Company has one pack today, `packs/hr-doo`, for a Croatian d.o.o. The owner wants a paušalni obrt, an obrt and a j.d.o.o. supported too. Their tax rules differ, so each needs research from the law before a plan: which of the d.o.o.'s classes, figures and screens apply, and what a form has that a d.o.o. does not.

A j.d.o.o. may share most of `hr-doo`, while an obrt and a paušalni obrt pay income tax rather than profit tax, so they are likely packs of their own. That reading is unchecked.
