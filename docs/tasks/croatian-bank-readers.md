# Readers for every Croatian bank

Today Spending imports Revolut, and bank sync goes through Enable Banking. The owner's long-term plan is a parser or integration for every Croatian bank, so a person at any bank can bring statements without a key. Until then, the model can read a statement into the import's dry run.
