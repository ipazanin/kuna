# Co-ownership and co-borrowers

How the Assets and Loans apps count your part of a flat, a car or anything else you own with someone, and of a bank loan you took out with someone. Kuna keeps your part as a percent with two decimals on the asset or the loan, with dated changes after it, and every total counts only your part. The acts were read in Narodne novine on 18 November 2026.

## Your part of an asset

ZV Art. 36(1) (NN 91/96): "Ako više osoba ima neku stvar u svome vlasništvu tako da svakoj pripada po dio toga prava vlasništva, računski određen razmjerom prema cijelom pravu vlasništva te stvari, sve su one suvlasnici te stvari, a dijelovi prava vlasništva koji im pripadaju njihovi su suvlasnički dijelovi."

So a co-owner's part is a fraction of the whole right of ownership, such as a half or a third. Kuna holds it as a percent of the whole and counts your part of a value as the whole value times that percent. Mark: `confirmed-nn`.

ZV Art. 37(4) makes each co-owner the owner of the ideal part that matches their share, and Art. 38(2) splits the fruits, costs and burdens of the whole thing by the shares unless the co-owners agree otherwise. Mark: `confirmed-nn`.

Screen: the asset page's line about your part, and the Assets Overview and Net worth, which count only your part.

## When nothing is agreed

ZV Art. 36(2): "U sumnji koliki su suvlasnički dijelovi, smatra se da su jednaki." Two co-owners who never fixed their shares own a half each. Mark: `confirmed-nn`.

ZV Art. 57(4): "U sumnji sudjeluju li osobe u suvlasništvu ili zajedničkom vlasništvu neke stvari, smatra se da je to suvlasništvo." Joint ownership without set parts exists only on the ground of a law (Art. 57(2)). Mark: `confirmed-nn`.

For spouses, ObZ Art. 36(3) (NN 103/15): "Bračni drugovi su u jednakim dijelovima suvlasnici bračne stečevine, ako nisu drukčije ugovorili." A flat bought from earnings during the marriage is half yours unless the spouses agreed otherwise. Mark: `confirmed-nn`.

Kuna does not presume a share. A blank part means the asset is all yours, and you type a half when that is what you own. Mark: `reading`.

## Your part of a loan

ZOO Art. 41(2) (NN 35/05): "Kad u nekoj djeljivoj obvezi ima više dužnika, dug se dijeli među njima na jednake dijelove, ako nije određena drukčija podjela, i svaki od njih odgovara za svoj dio duga." A money debt is divisible, so two borrowers with nothing else agreed each owe half and answer only for their half. Mark: `confirmed-nn`.

ZOO Art. 42 presumes the borrowers are jointly liable only for a debt from a commercial contract: "Kad ima više dužnika u nekoj djeljivoj obvezi nastaloj trgovačkim ugovorom, oni odgovaraju vjerovniku solidarno, osim ako su ugovaratelji izrijekom otklonili solidarnu odgovornost." ZOO Art. 14(2) makes a commercial contract one that traders make among themselves. A housing loan to two people as consumers is not one, so the law alone does not make them jointly liable. Mark: `reading`.

Kuna counts what falls on you between the borrowers, which is the part you type. Mark: `reading`.

Screen: the loan page's line about your part, and the Loans Overview, its home card and Net worth, which count only your part.

## When the bank can ask either borrower

ZOO Art. 43(1): "Svaki dužnik solidarne obveze odgovara vjerovniku za cijeli dug i vjerovnik može zahtijevati njegovo ispunjenje od koga hoće sve dok ne bude potpuno ispunjen, ali kad jedan dužnik ispuni dug, obveza prestaje i svi se dužnici oslobađaju." Where the borrowers are jointly liable, the bank can ask either of them for the whole balance. Mark: `confirmed-nn`.

Whether they are jointly liable turns on the loan contract, since the law presumes it only for a commercial contract (see the heading above). A bank's housing loan contract usually names the borrowers as joint debtors, but no contract was read. So the loan page says the bank can ask either borrower for all of it when the contract makes them jointly liable, and does not say it as a rule of law. Mark: `unconfirmed`.

Screen: the loan page's line that the bank can ask either borrower for all of it.

## Between the borrowers

ZOO Art. 52(1): "Dužnik koji je ispunio obvezu ima pravo zahtijevati od svakog sudužnika da mu naknadi dio duga koji pada na njega." A borrower who paid more than their part can claim the rest back from the other. Mark: `confirmed-nn`.

ZOO Art. 53(1): "Ako nije što drugo ugovoreno ili inače ne proizlazi iz pravnih odnosa sudionika u poslu, na svakog dužnika dolazi jednak dio duga." Art. 53(2) puts the whole debt on one borrower when the joint debt was taken out only in that borrower's interest. Mark: `confirmed-nn`.

So the part Kuna takes off your Net worth is the part that falls on you between the borrowers, a half unless you agreed otherwise. The bank's whole balance stays on the loan page, since that is what the bank can ask for under a joint contract. Instalments stay whole, as the bank takes them from the account they are paid from.

## Worked example

Made-up figures. A flat worth 240,000.00 owned at 33.33% counts 240,000.00 × 33.33% = 79,992.00. A loan with a balance of 150,000.00 at 50% counts 75,000.00 owed. What is yours after the loan is 79,992.00 − 75,000.00 = 4,992.00.

A flat bought at a half in 2020 and bought out on 1 March 2026 counts a half on every day before 1 March 2026 and the whole from that day.

## Open points

- No bank's housing loan contract was read, so whether co-borrowers are joint debtors is not confirmed. The loan page words the bank's claim on the whole balance as resting on the contract.
- Reading a consumer housing loan as no commercial contract under ZOO Art. 14(2) is a reading. A court or a later act could read it otherwise.
- Kuna does not check that the parts of every co-owner add up to the whole, since it records only yours.
