# Acts and issues

Every act and bylaw these docs cite: its abbreviation, its title, the Narodne novine (NN) issues, the page its text was read from, and how it was read. Every other file cites acts by these abbreviations. Law as researched on 4 November 2026.

## How to read the tables

- **NN issues** are the act and its amendments, oldest first.
- **Read** says how the text was read: the NN or Official Journal text, a consolidated text with its amendment list checked, or not at all. An act read only in a consolidated text supports no mark higher than `confirmed-consolidated`. See [confidence and citations](confidence-and-citations.md).
- zakon.hr was current through NN 119/26 on 4 November 2026. The titles of NN 120/26 to 126/26 were checked for amendments, and none touched these acts.
