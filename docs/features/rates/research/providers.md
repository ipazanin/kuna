# Exchange rate APIs for Kuna

Researched 6 and 7 November 2026.

## Recommendation

Keep the ECB as the only source for anything that enters tax or company books. Add Frankfurter v2 as a second, keyless source for display and for currencies the ECB lacks. Do not add a keyed provider yet.

Why:

- Tax needs HNB's middle rate on the payment day (ZPDoh Art. 12(8)). The HNB list carries the ECB rate of the day, so the ECB feed already reproduces it for the 13 currencies HNB publishes. The repo's `docs/law/investments/hnb-rates-and-settlement.md` documents this and Kuna's `hnbRate.ts` is built on it.
- HNB's own API has no CORS headers, so a browser cannot call it. Frankfurter v2 exposes HNB's list as a provider (`providers=HNB`, from 2023-01-01) with CORS open, if Kuna ever wants HNB's list directly.
- Frankfurter v2 needs no key, allows browser calls, has no stated quota, and covers about 200 currencies (161 fiat, 35 crypto, 4 metals) with history. No keyed free tier matches that. The keyed ones are 100 to 1,500 requests a month and mostly lack a range call.
- Blended (default) Frankfurter rates are not official. Its own license page says to check tax and accounting figures against the provider. Its blended USD for 6 Nov was 1.1219 while the ECB provider gave 1.1165, so never mix the two in one calculation.
- If the owner still wants a keyed all-currencies source, the best fit is ExchangeRate-API free (1,500 requests a month, 165 currencies, CORS open, personal use allowed) for current rates, and Open Exchange Rates free (1,000 a month, USD base, one call per past day) for history. Both are weaker than Frankfurter for this app. Details below.

Worth opening in Kuna: `src/shared/rates/infrastructure/ecbRates.ts` (the ECB fetch, where a second source would plug in), `src/apps/investments/prices/infrastructure/vendors/priceVendors.ts` (the vendor list), and `tax/domain/hnbRate.ts`.

## Croatian law and practice

Personal investment income, confirmed:

- ZPDoh Art. 12(8): income in a foreign currency is converted to euros at the middle rate HNB publishes on the day of payment. Loaded from zakon.hr on 7 Nov 2026. Art. 11(3) sets the cash principle. The PravPDoh Art. 11(4) repeat is in the repo's law doc, not reloaded by me.
- The repo shows HNB's list published on day D carries the ECB rate of D, checked on 4 Nov against USD. I rechecked one day: Frankfurter's HNB provider gives USD 1.1216 for 3 Nov and the ECB gives 1.1179. HNB's `datum_primjene` of 3 Nov is 1.1216, which equals the ECB rate of Mon 2 Nov. So the two readings differ in practice. This is the open point already in the repo doc. Frankfurter dates HNB by `datum_primjene`, Kuna's ECB path dates by publication day.
- HNB publishes 13 foreign currencies. A payment in a currency outside them has no HNB rate. What the law then requires is unconfirmed. The ECB list has 30 but lacks BAM, which HNB has (1.95583, fixed rate).

Company books, VAT:

- Porezna uprava VAT FAQ (loaded 7 Nov 2026): convert at HNB's middle rate on the day the VAT obligation arises. The taxpayer may instead use the ECB rate published on that day. The page still speaks of kuna, so its current wording is dated.

Company books, accounting and profit tax, unconfirmed:

- Search hits say HNB's middle rate is the practice for the spot rate (HSFI, Zakon o deviznom poslovanju Art. 45) and that after the euro HNB's euro rates apply for statistical, accounting and tax reporting unless a rule says otherwise. I could not load the Zakon o uvođenju eura text or a current ZoR or ZPD article. Treat as unconfirmed and read the Narodne novine text before relying on it.

HNB API: documented at https://api.hnb.hr/ (loaded). JSON default, XML optional, repeat `valuta`, no limits, terms or CORS stated. Measured: no CORS headers, so calls from the PWA fail. It does set cookies. A static GitHub Pages app cannot use it without a proxy.

## Unconfirmed

- Open Exchange Rates: HTTPS on free, time series on free, total currency count. Founding year is from memory.
- Fixer: free base currency and rate source. Terms not read. exchangerate.host: base on free is inferred from the plan table.
- CurrencyAPI: terms page 404. Whether `dates` is allowed on free.
- Twelve Data: credit cost per forex call, free history depth, forex endpoints on Basic. Not tested because it needs a key.
- Alpha Vantage: whether `outputsize=full` is open to a free key. Terms not read.
