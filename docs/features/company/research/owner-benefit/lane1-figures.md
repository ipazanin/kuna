# Owner benefit, lane 1: figures and data model

How the owner and tax figures are built today, where a private-use share of a company cost would enter each one, a worked example per reading of 'capital gains taxes', and the data model options for a category default with a per-row override.

Read-only research on the HEAD copy at 7128551. Every path is relative to that copy. Export checks come from two scratch scripts that print counts and slugs only, run with `node --import scripts/support/typescriptResolve.mjs <script> <export>` from the HEAD copy.

## The short answer

- The cash view already keeps "total stays the same" for free. Net is income less operating, taxes and owner pay (`src/features/workbook/domain/bucketTotals.ts:108-118`). Moving 600 from operating to owner pay leaves cost and net unchanged. Owner earns rises by the share, since it is owner pay plus net (`bucketTotals.ts:117`).
- Whether profit before tax, corporate tax and dividend capacity move depends on what the share is in tax terms. The app cannot tell. Read as "my own shadow figure, the accountant books the whole cost", nothing on Year moves and the tax saving is a comparison figure. Read as "private use the accountant is told about", the cost is added back for corporate tax and 36% capital income tax falls due. That is the opposite of a saving.
- No existing concept carries a percentage. A split moves a fixed amount to another category, and it is refused on a row an asset claims (`src/packs/hr-doo/domain/countedSplits.ts:14-25`), which is exactly the laptop.
- Putting the share into an `owner-pay` category, as a split part would, breaks three places that select owner pay by category: the forecast hands those categories to the pay scenario (`src/packs/hr-doo/domain/forecast/forecastYear.ts:437-446`), the scenarios strip them out of expenses as pay to be replaced (`src/packs/hr-doo/domain/scenarios/scenarioCompany.ts:53-62`), and the accrual cost line defaults them to personnel costs (`src/packs/hr-doo/domain/accrual/costLines.ts:59-61`).

## 2. Where a share would enter, by reading of "capital gains taxes"

### The readings

| Reading                             | What it means                                                                                                                                              | Source                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| ----------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A. Dividend tax avoided             | The private 30% was paid as a company cost, so the owner got it without the 12% dividend tax a payout would carry.                                         | 12% on dividends: ZPDoh Art. 70(19), "plaća se po odbitku, po stopi od 12%" (zakon.hr, quoted in `owner-pay.ts:982` and seen again today).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| B. Corporate tax avoided            | The cost lowered profit, so 10% less corporate tax.                                                                                                        | `hr.cit.rate` (`profit-tax.ts:225-243`).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| C. Both                             | The whole route compared with a dividend. Per 100.00 of company money, a dividend leaves 79.20, private use 100.00.                                        | Same as A and B. The Pay tab's dividend route computes exactly this (`marginalEuro.ts:48-56`).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| D. Declared private use by a member | Croatian law taxes it as capital income at 36%, and the cost is not deductible. This is the legal meaning of "porez na dohodak od kapitala" for this case. | ZPDoh Art. 66(1) "izuzimanja imovine i korištenje usluga od strane članova trgovačkih društava za njihove privatne potrebe", Art. 70(2) 36% by withholding, Art. 70(3) by the PD filing date (zakon.hr, loaded today through the fetch tool, which returns extracts, so the wording is fragments). Already in the ruleset as `hr.pit.hidden-distribution`, `costDeductibleForCompany: false` (`owner-pay.ts:990-1012`), which nothing computes yet.                                                                                         |
| E. Benefit in kind through payroll  | The owner is also an employee, so private use may be salary in kind: income tax and contributions, and the cost stays deductible.                          | ZPDoh Art. 21(3) "Primicima u naravi smatraju se korištenje zgrada, prometnih sredstava, povoljnije kamate" (zakon.hr, loaded today). ZPD Art. 7(2): no add-back "kada se sukladno Zakonu o porezu na dohodak obračunava i plaća porez na dohodak", except points 9 and 12 (zakon.hr, loaded today). RRiF 8/2026 (Božina, abstract only, rest paywalled) says for cars the benefit "najčešće se u praksi promatra kroz dohodak od nesamostalnog rada". That a laptop or a consultation follows the car practice is my analogy, unconfirmed. |

ZPD Art. 7(1)(12) adds back costs "utvrđene u postupku nadzora" that arose "u svezi skrivenih isplata dobiti, te izuzimanja dioničara, članova društva", and 7(2) keeps that add-back even when income tax was paid. So an undeclared share found in a tax audit costs the add-back plus the 36%. Whether a declared and taxed withdrawal stays deductible under 7(2) contradicts the ruleset's `costDeductibleForCompany: false`. That is a question for the accountant.

My reading of the owner's sentence is A or C: a shadow figure for the owner, with the accountant's books unchanged. Under A, B and C nothing on Year moves. Under D and E the books change, and the app would only model what the accountant decides.

| Owner take, owner cost                        | Yes, up by the share | A new line beside the categories. Cost per euro received falls, since no tax sits on the share.                                         |
| Effective tax rate                            | No                   | Taxes bucket and revenue unchanged.                                                                                                     |
| Accrual profit, cost lines, PD, corporate tax | No                   | The accountant books the whole cost on its usual konto. Year, Accountant and Statements stay matched.                                   |
| Dividend capacity, dividend tax               | No                   |                                                                                                                                         |
| Pay scenarios                                 | No, unless chosen    | The share is the same in every scenario. Shown, it would add to net to you and to profit before owner pay alike, so the identity holds. |

### The worked example

Made-up 2026: revenue 50,000, other operating costs 8,000, a laptop paid 2,000 on 15 March and put in use that day (VAT left aside), AI consultations 1,200, owner pay 20,000, owner-pay taxes 5,000, corporate tax advances 1,000. Laptop at 30%, AI at 50%. The laptop is a computer at 50% a year from the month after use (`profit-tax.ts:341-364`), so 750 in 2026, 1,000 in 2027 and 250 in 2028.

Cash view, 2026, the share taken on the payment:

| Line                         | Before | After                            |
| ---------------------------- | ------ | -------------------------------- |
| Income                       | 50,000 | 50,000                           |
| Operating                    | 11,200 | 10,000 (less 600 laptop, 600 AI) |
| Taxes                        | 6,000  | 6,000                            |
| Owner pay                    | 20,000 | 21,200                           |
| Cost                         | 37,200 | 37,200                           |
| Net                          | 12,800 | 12,800                           |
| Owner earns                  | 32,800 | 34,000                           |
| Owner take                   | 20,000 | 21,200                           |
| Owner cost per euro received | 1.25   | 1.24                             |
| Effective tax rate           | 12.0%  | 12.0%                            |

Accrual 2026, same company: expenses 8,000 + depreciation 750 + AI 1,200 + owner pay 20,000 + its taxes 5,000 = 34,950. Profit before tax 15,050, corporate tax 1,505, after tax 13,545, all distributable, dividend tax at the most 1,625.40.

## 5. Data model options

The share needs a default per category and an override per bank row, per owner advance, and in practice per asset.

**Default per category**

| Option                                          | Cost                                                                                                                | Consequence                                                                                                     |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A `private_share` column on `categories`        | One column, one field on the core `Category` (`src/features/workbook/domain/workbook.ts:20-31`), one editor column. | Plainest. Core then holds a value only the pack's figures read, which the pack seam of wave 12b tries to avoid. |
| A pack kind keyed by category slug, its own tab | One tab, one kind, a slug reference the domain checks.                                                              | Clean ownership, one more tab the owner keeps in step with categories.                                          |

**Override per row**

| Option                                                | Cost                                                                                                                                                                                                      | Consequence                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| ----------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A split part                                          | None new.                                                                                                                                                                                                 | Rejected: no percentage, refused on the laptop row, and the owner-pay category problems above.                                                                                                        |
| A column on `ledger`                                  | The ledger write rules widen beyond `category` and `flagged_by` (wave 12b plan, "Repositories").                                                                                                          | The Apps Script inserts ledger rows until Wave 13. A new column left blank reads as "use the default", so it is safe. No ledger id until Wave 13, and a pack fact sits in the core ledger.            |
| A pack kind with `id`, the row's key, `share`, `note` | One tab, one kind. The key is the split key now (date, account, row amount, row balance, `splitsTabReader.ts:86-90`), the ledger id after Wave 13. An advance is named by its `id` (wave 12b decision 5). | Same reference rules as splits, so both move to the ledger id together. A capital purchase is overridden on its bank row, and the share then reaches the assets that claim it. One way for every row. |

Computing it, whatever the storage: a pack function such as `privateUse(workbook, period)` that returns each shared row with its share and amount, like `loanBenefit`. The cash screens need the share moved from operating to owner pay. Two ways, and the owner's rule says pick one:

1. Give the cash screens their own rows: the presented rows with a private part in a dedicated owner-pay category, used by Overview, Analysis and Owner, while Year, the forecast and the scenarios keep today's rows. Every bucket and category figure follows by itself. Cost: `useFilteredLedger` feeds two views, and the Year screen must use the other one.
2. Keep one set of rows and add the share as its own figure: Owner, Taken adds a line, and Overview shows the move as a note. Cheaper, but Overview's operating and owner pay lines would not move, which is what the owner asked for.

I recommend option 1, the category default as a column on `categories`, and the override as a pack kind keyed like splits.

## 6. On the export

`equipment` is the only `capital-purchase` category, and every asset on the export has its payment found. Any laptop share therefore sits on asset-claimed rows, where a split is refused. The likely share candidates are `ai-services`, `equipment`, `telecom`, `software-subscriptions` and `travel`.

## Questions for the owner

1. **What does "capital gains taxes" mean here?** Read it as a figure only for you, with the books unchanged, and the app shows how much tax the share avoided compared with a dividend: 249.60 on the example. Read it as something the accountant books, and it is 36% capital income tax plus the cost added back for corporate tax, 552.00 or more on the example. I recommend the shadow figure, with a plain note on Owner that the accountant has not booked it.
2. **Which basis for the laptop?** Count the payment on the purchase day, 600 in 2026, or follow depreciation, 225, 300 and 75. I recommend the payment, since Owner, Taken and Overview count payments.
3. **Should Overview's operating and owner pay lines move, or only Owner?** Moving them means the cash screens read their own rows and Year keeps today's. Owner only is cheaper, but operating cost would still include your laptop share.
4. **Where should the category default live?** A column on `categories` is simplest. A pack tab keeps the pack's facts out of core.
5. **Should the pay scenarios show the share?** It is the same in every scenario, so it changes no comparison. I would leave them alone.
6. **Does a private share of an owner advance count?** For example an `ai-services` advance, where the company repaid you for something partly private.
7. **Ask the accountant: is VAT on the private share handled?** The input VAT on a private-use share is likely not deductible. I have not checked that rule, so it is unconfirmed.
