# Hub overview: research notes

What the Kuna home shows today, which H4 and H5 pieces exist, which cross-app figures already have one owner, how currencies behave, a first hub overview and the checks it needs.

Read only, against the Kuna repo at 7d73c5e (main, clean tree). Paths are relative to the repo. Roadmap documents are under `docs/`. No figures from the owner's files were read.

## In short

- The home is one card per app (`src/shell/home/HomeScreen.vue:14-22`). Each card asks its app for `figures()`, which returns figures, `opening`, `failed` or null (`src/shared/app/appContribution.ts:45-52`). Every app's cached copy is opened when the page starts, whether or not you visit the app (`src/shared/sync/application/autoSync.ts:70`).
- Of H4 (proposal, roadmap table, line 185), the tiles and the home at `#/` are built. The waiting slot exists as one line per card. Drills that name the app do not exist: the whole card is one link to the app's first screen.
- Every figure the owner listed already has one domain function, except two: income this year has two paths, and Loans' net is worked out inside a presentation helper. Owner take and dividend capacity live in the hr-doo pack, which the company's home code may not import. Nothing of H5 exists.
- `Money` is unit-less integer cents (`src/shared/money/money.ts:5`). No shared type carries a currency or converts between apps. Loans and Investments are fixed to euros in the language's format. Only Company has a currency setting. So the overview can sum Loans and Investments today, must check their formats match before it does, and must never sum Company.
- Plainest proposal: a "Your money" card at the top of the home, fed by one optional field on the existing home-card figures. Investments value plus what others owe you, less what you owe, with Company's cash shown beside it and never added. The sum rule lives in one pure function in `src/shared/app`.

## 1. What the home shows today and what it costs

### The contract

- `AppHomeCard` has an icon, a purpose line and `figures(): HomeFigures | 'opening' | 'failed' | null` (`src/shared/app/appContribution.ts:45-52`).
- `HomeFigures` is a list of amounts, the first leading the card, plus one `waiting` line or null (`appContribution.ts:37-42`). A `HomeAmount` is a label, plain cents, and optional sign and tone (`appContribution.ts:27-34`). It has no currency and no link.
- The shared helper `homeCardFigures` maps a store's state to the four answers (`src/shared/app/homeCardFigures.ts:5-16`). A workbook on screen always gives figures, even while a sync runs or after a sync failed (`:9-11`). With no workbook, `opening` or `syncing` gives `opening` (`:12-14`), `error` gives `failed`, anything else gives null (`:15`).
- `HomeAppCard` skips figures while the app is in its first run and shows its purpose with a Set up badge (`src/shell/home/HomeAppCard.vue:19-20`, `:50-53`). It shows a pulsing bar for `opening` (`:37`), a sentence for `failed` (`:38`, words at `src/shell/home/words.ts:9`), and otherwise the amounts in the app's own money format (`:39-47`) with the waiting line as a badge (`:48`). The whole card is one link to `entryPath(app)` (`:22`, `:26-27`), which is the first run or the app's first screen (`src/shell/routing/appRegistry.ts:26-28`).

### What loading costs

- All three apps are imported and installed before the page mounts (`src/main.ts:19-22`). Each `app.ts` imports its home card, so the card's dependencies load at start. For Investments that is the whole portfolio engine through `usePortfolio`.
- Every store's `open()` runs as the page starts, through the auto sync (`src/shared/sync/application/autoSync.ts:70`, started at `src/shell/sync/keepPageSynced.ts:16`). `open()` reads only this browser's copy, one Dexie database per file (`src/shared/storage/infrastructure/workbookCache.ts:17-28`), and never Drive (`src/shared/storage/application/createWorkbookStore.ts:240-261`). So the home shows every app's figures from the cache at once, whether or not you open the app.
- A Drive read happens only through the page's one sync: by itself when due, at most every 10 minutes, with a live token and auto sync on (`autoSync.ts:5`, `:24-31`), or from a tap. While it runs the status is `syncing` (`createWorkbookStore.ts:279`), but cached figures stay on the card.
- Opening with no cache, the first time on a device, shows `opening` until the first sync lands. The fake `?loans=opening` and `?investments=opening` hold the read to show this (`src/apps/loans/workbook/infrastructure/fake/seedFakeLoans.ts:42`, `src/apps/investments/workbook/infrastructure/fake/seedFakeInvestments.ts:56-60`).
- Failed: `failed` shows only when there is no cached copy and the read failed. With a cached copy, a failed sync keeps showing the old figures (`homeCardFigures.ts:9-11`), and the failure shows in the sync control instead (`src/shared/sync/presentation/pageSync.ts:142-151`).
- Not set up: an app whose store has no choice and no workbook is in its first run (`src/apps/company/app.ts:70`, `src/apps/loans/app.ts:52`, `src/apps/investments/app.ts:83`). The card offers Set up.
- Signed out: Sign out forgets the token and the device's grant (`src/shared/google/googleAuth.ts:250-269`, from `src/shell/settings/SyncCard.vue:19-24`). The choice and the cached copies stay, so the cards keep showing the last figures. On the next open with a Drive file chosen and auto sync on, the page still tries one silent trip to Google (`src/main.ts:24`), because the granted scopes fall back to `drive.file` when none are remembered (`googleAuth.ts:84-86`) and Sign out clears the silent-refusal key too (`src/shared/google/googleSession.ts:57-61`). What Google answers after the revoke is unverified. Either way the home shows this browser's copies until a sync lands.
- Browser storage versus Drive: a file kept in the browser is read from Dexie and never syncs (`createWorkbookStore.ts:131-134`, `:244-247`). Its figures are always current. A Drive file's figures are as fresh as the last read.
- Each card calls `figures()` inside its own computed (`HomeAppCard.vue:20`). Loans recomputes every loan's plan on each call (`loansHomeCard.ts:18-22`). Cheap today, but an overview that calls `figures()` a second time doubles it.

### Why the company figures cannot reach the home today

- The shell may not import an app (`tests/importBoundaries.spec.ts:137-141`), and no app may import another (`:143-145`). Everything crosses through `AppContribution`.
- Company code outside the pack may not import pack code (`importBoundaries.spec.ts:147-149`). The company home card is outside the pack, so it cannot call `ownerTake`, `dividendCapacity` or `netOwed`.
- The pack seam has no slot for home or hub figures (`src/apps/company/packs/packContribution.ts:47-64`). It has `waitingJobs` for the Overview only.

### What the roadmap meant for the company, and H5

- Proposal, decision 4: personal apps read company facts live, never copies. The company app publishes typed facts such as owner payments and owner loans (proposal, "The owner's decisions, 2026-10-22"). The target diagram has `facts.ts (published, H5)` read through one translation file per reader (proposal, the target diagram and the pattern table).
- Roadmap: H5 is `apps/company/facts.ts` with owner payments, the owner loan book and net owed, and the dashboard shows net owed (proposal, roadmap table, line 186). Lane 3 lists owner take, net owed and company cash as computed views the dashboard reads, with "Company and personal accounts stay in two lists" (`docs/architecture/research/hub-lane3-apps.md:100`, `:104`, `:110`, and the dashboard rule at `:129`).
- Whether net worth counts the owner's stake in the company was left open (proposal, "Decisions left to each app's own discussion", and lane 3 question 3). The Investments research recommended showing Company apart until the owner decides (`docs/features/investments/research/investments/lane2-kuna-fit.md`). No answer is recorded in the project plan.

## 4. Currencies

- `Money` is integer cents with no unit (`src/shared/money/money.ts:5`). `sumMoney` adds any cents (`:46-52`). `HomeAmount.cents` is a plain number (`appContribution.ts:29`).
- `CurrencyCode` exists in the kernel with `EURO` as "the only base currency" (`src/shared/money/currencyCode.ts:2-5`). Conversion exists only inside Investments, at ECB rates from its prices file (`src/apps/investments/prices/domain/rateBook.ts:9-26`). There is no shared money-with-currency type and no shared rate source.
- Each app gives its own `MoneyFormat` (`appContribution.ts:74-75`). Company reads its currency and number locale from its settings (`src/apps/company/app.ts:67`, the setting at `src/apps/company/settings/domain/settings.ts:42` and `:144`, checked at `:773-782`). Loans is fixed to `DEFAULT_MONEY_FORMAT` until it has settings (`src/apps/loans/app.ts:49-50`). Investments is fixed to it because every figure leaves the app in euros (`src/apps/investments/app.ts:80-81`). `DEFAULT_MONEY_FORMAT` is EUR in the language's locale (`src/shared/money/moneyFormat.ts:28`). Loans has no currency field anywhere (no `currency` in `src/apps/loans`).
- The home formats each card in its own app's format (`HomeAppCard.vue:44`), and the page keeps the default outside an app (`src/shell/App.vue:72-73`).
- What happens if two files differ today: nothing breaks, because nothing sums across apps. If a sum were added naively, a Company in USD would be added to euro cents with no warning. Loans and Investments cannot differ today, since neither has a setting.
- Conclusion: there is nothing to convert with outside Investments, and converting Company is out anyway because it is never summed. The overview should sum only when every personal part reports the same currency, and otherwise show each part in its own format with a plain line saying they were not added up. That case is unreachable today and is a spec, not a screen.

## 5. Proposal: a first hub overview

### What it shows (plainest version)

One card at the top of the home, above the app cards, titled something like "Your money":

- **Net**, the lead figure: Investments value plus what others owe you, less what you owe.
- **The parts** under it: Investments value, Owed to you (only when above zero, as the Loans card does), You owe in all. Each part links to its app's Overview, which is H4's "drills name the app".
- **A line naming what the net leaves out**: bank accounts and property, which no app keeps yet, and holdings without a price when Investments has any (`portfolioFigures.ts:46-50`).
- **Company beside it, never added**: "The company's cash" in the company's own format, with a short line that it is the company's money and not part of the net. It links to the Company Overview.

The app cards stay below as they are. Gain, income, tax dates and the review line stay on the cards, so the overview repeats nothing but the three parts it adds up.

### Where the figures come from (one owner each)

| Figure on the overview | Owner                                                            | How it reaches the shell                |
| ---------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| Investments value      | `portfolioFigures` through `usePortfolio`                        | Investments card's figures              |
| You owe, owed to you   | `owedTotals`                                                     | Loans card's figures                    |
| Net                    | one new pure function in `src/shared/app`, such as `personalNet` | called by the shell's home              |
| Company cash           | `companyHomeFigures`, which calls `latestBalances`               | Company card's figures, its lead amount |

Contract change, the smallest that keeps the rule in the types: `HomeFigures` gains an optional `yours` field, `{ has: Money, owes: Money }` with links, which only Loans and Investments fill. Company never fills it, so its cash cannot enter the sum by mistake. The app that keeps the company's books says so with one constant on its contribution, such as `money: 'personal' | 'company'`, so the overview knows which card to show apart without reading app ids. The plan should check whether `yours` alone is enough and drop the constant if it is.

`HomeScreen` asks each app for `figures()` once and hands the answer to both its card and the overview, instead of each card calling it in its own computed (`HomeAppCard.vue:20`).

Loans would move its card's computation into a domain function beside Company's (`src/apps/company/home/domain/homeFigures.ts` is the model), so the card and the overview take the same totals and a spec covers them.

### How it behaves

| A personal app is...                                                                                           | The overview                                                                                                                                                             |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Not set up (first run)                                                                                         | Adds nothing for it and says so in the leave-out line, with a link to its Set up. A net from Investments alone, or loans alone, is still true.                           |
| Set up with nothing to show (`null`, such as Investments with no transactions, `investmentsHomeCard.ts:37-39`) | Adds nothing for it.                                                                                                                                                     |
| Opening (no cached copy yet, or Investments waiting for its prices)                                            | Shows the same pulsing placeholder as a card (`HomeAppCard.vue:37`) for the net until every personal app answers. With a cache this lasts the time of an IndexedDB read. |
| Failed (no cached copy and the read failed)                                                                    | Withholds the net and names the app that did not open, with a link into it. Shows the parts that did open.                                                               |
| Showing a cached copy while signed out, or after a failed sync                                                 | Shows the net from this browser's copies, the same as the cards. The sync control already says the read is old or failed (`pageSync.ts:142-151`).                        |
| In another currency than the other personal app                                                                | Refuses to add up and shows each part in its own format with a line saying why. Unreachable today.                                                                       |

Company not set up, opening or failed only changes the company line, never the net.

### Gotchas the plan has to carry

- **A housing loan without the home.** Bank loans are in Loans, property is not in any app (H8 is not built). The net counts the loan and not what it bought, so it can be a large minus. This is decision 1 below.
- **Two "todays".** Loans uses the device's day (`src/shared/calendar/civilDate.ts:21-24`), Investments the day in Zagreb (`pricesUpdate.ts:17-22`) and values at the newest close. They differ only around midnight abroad or after a missed price fetch, but the net is "as of" two dates. The leave-out line can name the close when it is not today.
- **A loan between the owner and the company.** If one is typed into Loans as an informal loan, and H5 later adds the company's `netOwed` to the net, it would be counted twice. Not a problem while Company stays apart, but H5 has to decide which app owns that loan.
- **The proposal's word was "net worth".** It is better not to call this figure net worth until property and bank accounts are in, since it leaves both out.

### Decisions only the owner can make

**1. Show a net before your home and bank accounts are in Kuna?**
The home opens every morning on this card. Say your investments are worth a modest amount and your housing loan is several times that. The net then reads as a large minus, though the flat behind the loan is worth more.

- _Show the net with a plain line naming what it leaves out._ You see one figure that moves with your investments and repayments. Cost: the figure looks worse than your real position until H8 adds property.
- _Show the three parts side by side with no net._ Nothing misleads, but the card repeats what the Loans and Investments cards already show, so it adds little.
- _Wait for H8 and build the overview with property in it._ The first net you see is a real one. Cost: no hub overview for several waves.
  Recommendation: show the net with the leave-out line, and call it something like "Investments less loans" rather than net worth.

**2. What does Company show beside it?**

- _Cash on hand only, kept apart._ Nothing new in the company app. Cost: none beyond the card.
- _Also what you took out this year, dividend capacity and what you and the company owe each other._ The card answers "how much could I still take out". Cost: a new home slot in the hr-doo pack, a year forecast worked out on every home open for dividend capacity, and most of H5 pulled into this wave.
  Recommendation: cash on hand only now. The owner figures come with H5, which publishes them properly.

**3. On the home, or on a screen of its own?**

- _At the top of the home, above the app cards._ You see it every time Kuna opens. Cost: the home gets longer on a phone, and the cards move down by one card's height.
- _Its own screen, linked from the home._ The home stays a list of apps. Cost: a route, a menu entry and one more tap to see the figure.
  Recommendation: the top of the home, as your request said.

**4. When Loans or Investments has not opened, show a net from the other alone?**
Say Investments opened from this browser's copy but Loans failed to read on a new device.

- _Withhold the net and say which app did not open._ You never see a figure that is wrong by the missing app. Cost: no net until that app opens.
- _Show the net from what opened, marked as partial._ You always see a number. Cost: on that day the number leaves out your loans and looks far better than it is.
  Recommendation: withhold on a failure, show a placeholder while opening, and count an app that is not set up as nothing.

**5. Does the next loan payment belong here?**

- _Under You owe on the overview, as a short caption with date and amount._ One place for "what goes out next". Cost: one more line in the card.
- _On the Loans card's waiting line, which is empty today._ The overview stays three figures. Cost: a bank instalment is not a job, so it would sit in a slot the other apps use for things to do.
- _Leave it out for now._ Cost: none.
  Recommendation: a caption under You owe, from `upcomingPayments(..., 1)`.

Not decisions, stated as defaults: the overview refuses to add up personal parts in different currencies, and Company is never added in. Income this year and total gain stay on the Investments card.

## 6. What the checks need

### Specs (Vitest, in `tests/`)

- The new pure function in `src/shared/app`: adds `has` and subtracts `owes` over the personal apps, skips null and first-run apps, waits on `opening`, withholds on `failed`, refuses on different currencies, and never reads a company part. One spec file, such as `tests/personalNet.spec.ts`.
- A Loans home figures domain function, specced on the fake sets like `tests/companyHomeFigures.spec.ts`, with `owedTotals` still the one owner (`tests/owedTotals.spec.ts` stays).
- Investments: a spec that the `yours` part equals `portfolioFigures(...).value`, reusing `tests/fixtures/investments.ts`.
- `tests/importBoundaries.spec.ts` should pass unchanged, which proves the overview goes through the contribution.
- New words in `src/shell/home/words.ts` need English and Croatian (`Translation<typeof en>`), which `tests/words.spec.ts` and `tests/englishLeft.spec.ts` cover.

### Fake builds

- Seeds come from the query string per app: `?fake=` for Company (`src/apps/company/app.ts:48-51`), `?loans=` (`src/apps/loans/app.ts:32-35`), `?investments=` and `?prices=` (`src/apps/investments/app.ts:55-59`). A home with all three set up is `?loans=sample&investments=sample` on the default Company fake.
- Existing variants cover not set up (no query), opening (`loans=opening`, `investments=opening`) and late prices (`prices=late-prices`). `investments=binned` keeps a cached copy and fails the sync, so it shows figures, not `failed`.

Two notions of "profit left in the company" already exist. The tile's `leftInCompany` follows the Owner period and taxes each year through `dividendRoute`. `dividendCapacity` starts from the year's opening equity, the accountant's when the year-end tab has it. ### 2. One definition

**What the owner could take out now, after tax** is what reaches the owner's account if the company paid the owner everything it may pay today, in cash, with the open owner loan settled first:

1. **Profit available now** = retained earnings at the start of the year + this year's profit of its complete months after corporate tax − profit already paid out this year. This is the interim option's gross before the cash limit (`dividendCapacity.ts:318-329`).
2. **Capped by the year's own distributable profit**, so no part of it is a shortfall taxed as income. This is decision 1 below.
3. **Capped by the cash that may leave**: cash on the newest booking, less corporate tax due by the end of the month (overdue included), less unpaid pay for the last complete month (`dividendCapacity.ts:349-363`), less the corporate tax on the profit so far that is not yet due. The last part is new and is decision 3.
4. **Dividend tax** at 12% on that gross. **Net** = gross − tax.
5. **Open owner loan settled first**: set-off = the smaller of the net and what the owner owes (`owedByOwnerOn`). **Cash to the owner** = net − set-off.

Made-up example: retained earnings at the start of the year 20,000. Profit of the complete months 30,000 before tax, 27,000 after 10%. Nothing paid out yet. Profit available 47,000. Cash 40,000, with 1,000 of corporate tax due this month and the 1,500 of tax accrued on this year's profit not due yet, so 37,500 may leave. Gross 37,500, dividend tax 4,500, net 33,000. The owner owes 2,000 on a loan, so 2,000 settles it and 31,000 reaches the owner's account.

**What is legally a dividend, and when.**

- Retained earnings of years whose statements the members adopted can be paid now as a dividend, once the member decides. The ruleset reads ZTD Art. 406(1) as the entitlement to the year's profit plus retained earnings less losses brought forward (`ruleset/rules/company-law.ts:98-124`). I could not reload Art. 406, because the zakon.hr page for the act stops before the d.o.o. articles (the text received ran from Art. 1 to about Art. 300). So from this pass it is unconfirmed, and the ruleset marks it `confirmed-consolidated` from its 2026-10-18 load.
- This year's profit becomes a dividend only after the members adopt this year's statements, within eight months of the year end (ZTD Art. 441(1)(1) per `company-law.ts:178-200`, also not reloaded here).
- Paid during the year, it is an advance on profit (predujam dobiti). The tax law knows it. ZPD Art. 10.a, loaded 2026-11-04 from <https://www.zakon.hr/z/99/zakon-o-porezu-na-dobit> (consolidated up to NN 151/25): "Ako je tijekom poreznog razdoblja fizičkoj osobi isplaćen predujam dobiti, a istekom tog poreznog razdoblja ostvarena dobit nije dostatna za pokriće takvog predujma, tada se nastala razlika smatra primitkom koji se oporezuje u skladu sa Zakonom o porezu na dohodak." So the part the year's profit does not cover is taxed as income, not at 12%.
- PravPD Art. 35.a, loaded 2026-11-04 in the wording NN 123/10 added (<https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2010_11_123_3191.html>), paragraph 3: the company paying an advance "treba postupati sukladno posebnim propisima kojima se propisuje mogućnost isplate predujma dobiti", and pays it "do iznosa kojeg pokazuje privremeni račun dobiti i gubitka". Paragraph 4 requires a record of advances paid and settled, filed with the corporate tax return. I did not load the current consolidated wording, so later changes to 35.a are unchecked.
- Company law: ZTD Art. 221, "Plaćanje predujma dividende iz dijela neto dobiti", loaded 2026-11-04 from <https://www.zakon.hr/z/546/zakon-o-trgovackim-drustvima> through the fetch tool's summary. It sits in the joint-stock company chapter (Glava IV, dioničko društvo). It lets the articles authorise an advance after the business year ends, from an interim income statement, at most half of the profit. That is the d.d.'s rule. **Whether ZTD allows an interim payout during the year in a d.o.o. is unconfirmed.** PravPD 35.a(3) points to "special regulations that provide for the possibility", so the tax law does not settle it on its own. A binding opinion from Porezna uprava, or the d.o.o. articles from another primary copy (Narodne novine's consolidated text), would.

Made-up example of the shortfall: an advance of 15,000 paid in July on profit that ends the year at 12,000 after tax, with nothing retained. The 3,000 the year does not cover is taxed as the owner's income.

**One owner per rule.** Distributable profit, the interim profit and the shortfall stay in `dividendCapacity`. Dividend tax, net and set-off stay in `payoutOption`. The cash that may leave stays in `interimCash`. What the owner owes stays in `owedByOwnerOn`. The plainest change is a fourth option built by the same `payoutOption`, such as `now`, whose gross is the smallest of the interim gross, the year's distributable profit and the interim cash's largest payout, with `setsOff: true`. That reuses every rule above. The new rule, holding back corporate tax accrued and not yet due, belongs in `interimCash` (`dividendCapacity.ts:349-363`), which would also change the Year Pay tab's "cash allows" line. That is a visible change to an existing screen, so it needs the owner's yes with decision 3.

**Cost.** `dividendCapacity` needs this year's `forecastYear`, and `openingEquity` forecasts each earlier year back to the first period when the year-end tab lacks one (`dividendCapacity.ts:370-396`). The Company Overview already runs `forecastYear` for its corporate tax job (`presentation/packWaitingJobs.ts:71`), so the cost has a precedent. ### 4. Decisions only the owner can make

**1. Count this year's profit, or only the years already closed?**
Say it is November. Last year's statements are adopted with 20,000 retained, and this year's complete months show 27,000 after corporate tax.

- _Closed years only._ The figure is 20,000 less 12%, a payout that is a dividend once you decide it, with no tax risk. Cost: it ignores most of what the company earned this year, and it jumps once a year when the statements are adopted.
- _Closed years plus this year's profit so far, capped at the year's forecast distributable profit._ The figure is about 47,000 before the cash limit. The part from this year would be an advance on profit, which the tax law knows (ZPD 10.a) but whose company-law basis for a d.o.o. is unconfirmed. The cap keeps it inside what the year is expected to cover, so no part is taxed as income on the forecast.
- _The same without the cap._ A slightly larger figure in a strong first half. Cost: if the year ends weaker, the uncovered part is taxed as your income, not at 12%.
  Recommendation: closed years plus this year so far, capped, with a line that this year's part is an advance and the d.o.o. basis is unconfirmed.

**2. Show the cash you would receive, or the net including the loan it settles?**
The net comes to 33,000 and you owe the company 2,000 on a loan.

- _Cash to your account, 31,000, with "2,000 of it settles your loan" under it._ It answers "what reaches me". Cost: the figure drops whenever you borrow from the company, though your position does not change.
- _The net, 33,000, with the same line._ It answers "what I am worth out of the company". Cost: 2,000 of it never reaches your account.
  Recommendation: cash to your account, since the question is what you could take out.

**3. What cash does the company hold back first?**
The bank shows 40,000. 1,000 of corporate tax is due this month, and this year's profit so far has run up 1,500 of corporate tax that falls due later.

- _What Year does today: tax due this month and last month's unpaid pay._ 39,000 may leave. Cost: next spring's tax balance would come out of cash already paid to you.
- _Also the corporate tax on this year's profit not yet due._ 37,500 may leave. Cost: one new rule in `interimCash`, which also lowers the "cash allows" line on Year's Pay tab.
- _Also a buffer of some months of costs._ Safer still. Cost: a new setting for how many months, and a figure that is no longer only tax law.
  Recommendation: hold back the corporate tax not yet due, and leave a buffer out.

**4. Fix the interim option on Year, or keep the new figure apart from it?**
Today the Pay tab's interim payout taxes the whole profit and only warns when cash is short, and it does not settle loans.

- _Build the take-out figure as a fourth option beside it._ Year's Pay tab stays as it is, apart from the tax holdback if decision 3 says so. Cost: the Pay tab and the overview show two different "now" figures, and the plan has to say why.
- _Change the interim option itself to cap by cash and settle loans, and show it on the overview._ One "now" figure everywhere. Cost: the Pay tab's interim column changes, and the shortfall warning has to be redrawn around the capped amount.
  Recommendation: change the interim option itself, so Kuna has one figure for "pay out now" and not two.
