# Finance hub, lane 3: the future apps and their shared data

The first-version records of the personal loans, personal money and personal assets apps, their twins in the company app, the links between apps, the hub dashboard, storage per app and a difficulty ranking for the waves.

Code claims cite the HEAD copy at 7128551, with paths relative to it. Where Wave 12b changes a shape (records with ids, `Repository<R>`, the pack seam), this lane designs on the 12b target from [its plan](https://github.com/ipazanin/kuna/blob/cd621ab/docs/features/platform/storage-boundary-plan.md), not today's code. No real figure, name or IBAN is used.

## Summary

- **Personal money (expenses and budgeting) is the company app with other buckets.** Accounts, transactions, categories, rules, camt and tabular import, splits and category totals all have a near twin. The one deep obstacle is that buckets are a closed company list (`src/features/workbook/domain/bucket.ts:5`). A budget is the `plan` tab's category-by-month amount (`src/features/workbook/domain/plan.ts:21-28`) without the forecast around it.
- **Personal loans need one thing the company app lacks: a repayment schedule.** The owner-loan book matches repayments to loans after the fact (`src/packs/hr-doo/domain/ownerLoans.ts:74-83`) and has no rate, term or instalment. The day-count interest (`src/packs/hr-doo/domain/ownerLoanInterest.ts:53-58`) is reusable.
- **Personal assets v1 should be manual valuations, not depreciation.** The company asset is a tax record depreciated at statutory rates (`src/features/workbook/domain/assets.ts:9-28`, `src/packs/hr-doo/domain/assets/depreciationSchedule.ts:53-59`). A flat or a car is worth what you say it is on a date. Stocks are the hard part and can wait.
- **No link needs a cross-app write.** Every link is either a computed view (the dashboard, net worth, loan balances) or a reference by id. Copies are never needed. That matters because a Sheets `batchUpdate` is atomic only inside one spreadsheet.
- **Storage: keep the company Sheet as it is and add one personal Sheet with tabs per personal app.** The personal apps share accounts and the ledger, so their writes stay atomic. The company Sheet stays the company's record with no private tabs in it.
- **The hardest shared piece is not an app.** It is the app holding more than one workbook at once. Today the store, the cache key and the data-source choice all assume one (`src/features/workbook/infrastructure/workbookChoice.ts:10`, `src/features/workbook/application/workbookStore.ts:550`).

Two consequences for the shared pieces:

- **Every figure today drills into one ledger.** A `Figure` is an amount with filters for the Transactions screen (`src/features/filters/domain/drillTarget.ts:14-21`). A dashboard figure needs to name which app it opens, so the drill gains an app.
- **The dashboard reads published figures, not records.** Each app gives the hub a small typed summary (cash, net owed, loan balances, spending against budget). The hub never reads another app's tabs. That keeps 12b's boundary: an app's records are its own, as the pack's are after stage 8.

## 4. Storage per app

### What the code assumes today

- One workbook at a time. The choice is one `localStorage` key (`workbookChoice.ts:3-10`) of source `google` or `excel` (`workbook.ts:42-43`). 47 `src` files reference the store (`grep -rl 'useWorkbookStore\|useWorkbook(' src | wc -l`), and 19 branch on the source (`grep -rlE "source === '(google|excel)'|source !== '(google|excel)'" src | wc -l`).
- The offline copy is already one Dexie database per workbook id (`src/features/workbook/infrastructure/workbookCache.ts:17-26`). Several workbooks fit that without change.
- One write is one Sheets `batchUpdate` (`src/features/workbook/infrastructure/sheetsClient.ts:106-113`), atomic inside one spreadsheet only.
- Tab names are global: `ledger`, `categories`, `accounts`, `settings`, `rules` (README "The workbook format"). The Apps Script on the company Sheet reads them by those names.

### The options

| Option                                                                           | Sign-in and scopes                                                                                           | Sync and offline                                                                                 | Browser-workbook mode and export                                    | Company Sheet                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **One Sheet per app** (company, money, loans, assets)                            | `drive.file` covers each Sheet the app creates without a Picker. A Sheet made by hand needs one Picker pick. | One read per Sheet per sync. Four Sheets stay far below the read quota. One Dexie database each. | Each app picks Google or this browser on its own. One xlsx per app. | Untouched.                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **Tabs per app in one Sheet** (everything in the company Sheet)                  | One file, one pick                                                                                           | One read                                                                                         | One xlsx with every tab                                             | Personal ledger, loans and net worth live in the company's record, beside the Apps Script and whatever the accountant sees. Personal tabs need prefixed names, since `ledger` and `categories` are taken. |
| **A mix: the company Sheet, plus one personal Sheet with tabs per personal app** | Two files. The personal one created by the app needs no Picker.                                              | Two reads                                                                                        | Company and personal each pick their mode. Two xlsx files.          | Untouched.                                                                                                                                                                                                |

**Recommendation: the mix.** The personal apps share accounts and the ledger, and a write such as "add a loan and file its repayment category" touches both, so keeping them in one spreadsheet keeps one write atomic. The company Sheet stays the company's system of record with nothing private in it, and the company and personal sides never write together, so losing cross-Sheet atomicity costs nothing. Inside the personal Sheet the apps keep distinct tab names (`loans`, `loan-rates`, `assets`, `valuations`, `budget`), and 12b's kinds and repositories make the tab a mapping detail.

### Facts behind it

- `drive.file` scope, Google's Drive scopes page, loaded 2026-10-22 through the fetch tool's extract: "Create new Drive files, or modify existing files, that you open with an app", which also covers files "that the user shares with an app while using the Google Picker API or the app's file picker". The page lists it as non-sensitive: "Whenever possible, use non-sensitive scopes as they grant per-file access." <https://developers.google.com/workspace/drive/api/guides/api-specific-auth>
- The grant outlives the token, observed in the Wave 0 spike in Firefox: no re-pick per session (the project plan, "Wave 0 spike results").
- Batch atomicity, Google's batchUpdate guide, loaded 2026-10-22: "if one request is unsuccessful, none of the other (potentially dependent) changes are written." <https://developers.google.com/workspace/sheets/api/guides/batchupdate>
- Read quota, Google's Sheets limits page, loaded 2026-10-22: read requests "Per minute per user per project": 60, and the same for writes. <https://developers.google.com/workspace/sheets/api/limits>
- The app already creates Drive files under `drive.file`, folders and invoice PDFs (`src/features/invoicing/infrastructure/googleInvoiceDrive.ts:28-52`). Creating a Sheet is the same kind of call.
- The `drive.appdata` hidden folder exists ("contents are hidden from the user and from other Google Drive apps", <https://developers.google.com/workspace/drive/api/guides/appdata>, loaded 2026-10-22). It would hide your personal data from you in Drive, so it is not an option for a system of record.

### Suggested order

1. **Shell and several workbooks.** The company app becomes one app in the hub with no change to its figures. Builds on 12b stages 1 and 8.
2. **Personal money v1** in a new personal Sheet created by the app, or in this browser.
3. **Hub dashboard v1:** cash across both sides, spending against budget, what the owner took out, taxes due.
4. **Personal loans v1** with the schedule, then the repayment match.
5. **Personal assets v1** and net worth on the dashboard.
6. **Company-to-personal links:** salary, dividends and reimbursements matched, advances started from a personal row. After Wave 13 gives the company ledger ids.
7. **Stocks.**

Loans could go before personal money if you want loan balances on a dashboard first, since a schedule needs no ledger. It still needs step 1.

## Questions for the owner

1. **Where personal data lives.** I recommend the company Sheet unchanged plus one personal Sheet the app creates, with tabs for each personal app. Is that right, or do you want one Sheet per app?
2. **Which personal loans.** Bank loans with a schedule (a mortgage, a car loan), loans to and from family with no schedule, or both? For a bank loan, fixed rate only, or fixed then variable?
3. **The company in your net worth.** Should net worth count your stake in the company (its book equity, or what you could take out as a dividend), or show the company beside it and leave it out of the sum?
4. **Owner advances.** Once your personal ledger exists, should "Add advance" start from a personal payment you mark as paid for the company, or do you keep typing advances by hand?
5. **Budget shape.** One amount per category per month, as the `plan` tab has, or envelopes where an unspent amount carries into the next month?
6. **Who else opens the company Sheet.** If the accountant or anyone else does, that settles that nothing personal goes into it.
