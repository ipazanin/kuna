Privacy

Kuna is a web page that works out company finance figures in your browser. Updated 6 November 2026.

What the app asks Google for

Each permission is shown with the words Google uses for it on its consent screen, then what Kuna does with it. Where that screen lets you untick one, the app skips what needs it and shows "Allow access" in its sync control.

  • drive.file, "See, edit, create, and delete only the specific Google Drive files you use with this app". Always asked. It covers the files and folders you pick in Google's file picker and the files the app makes. The app reads and saves your books in kuna-company.json, the file it makes in a folder inside its own Kuna folder, and reads the names of that folder and of the backups beside the file. When you press Move in an app's Settings, it moves into the Kuna folder that file and what the app itself made: its backups, its prices file, receipt photos and statements it filed from your mail. Everything else stays, your accountant's files among them. When you make an invoice, it lists the folders inside the invoice folder you picked, and files the PDF there. When a sync files your bank's statement mail or a kind of your accountant's mail you switched on, it makes the files, and a missing year or month folder, in that mail's folder. When you switch on Keep the photo on Drive for a receipt, it makes the photo's file, and a Receipts folder when it is missing, beside your Spending file.
  • drive.readonly, "See and download all your Google Drive files". Asked only while you have the app read bank statements from a Drive folder or file your mail into one. Google's wording covers your whole Drive. The app uses it to read the bank statements in your statements folder that are not on the ledger yet, since drive.file cannot see files another app filed there. While it reads your mail, it also lists the names, dates and checksums of the files in the folders that mail goes into, to tell what is filed already. It reads nothing else with it and changes nothing.
  • drive.appdata, "See, create, and delete its own configuration data in your Google Drive". Asked only while a file of yours is on Drive and you saved a key, hold an instrument priced by a vendor that needs a key, or switched the assistant on for an app. It covers one hidden folder of Kuna's own in your Drive, which you do not see in Drive, which nobody can share and which no other app reads. The app keeps your keys there in one small file, so every browser you sign in to gets them. It also deletes there the keys files an older Kuna made, one per Investments file, once their keys are in the new file. It reads, writes and deletes nothing else with it.
  • gmail.readonly, "View your email messages and settings". Asked only while you name the address your bank mails from, or switch on a kind of your accountant's mail. Google's wording covers your whole mailbox. On each sync the app searches it for mail from those addresses whose subject starts with the words you set, reads each one's subject, date and attachment names, downloads a bank statement's XML to read its number and account, and downloads an attachment to file it or to compare it with a file of the same name in your Drive. It files your bank's new statements and the kinds of your accountant's mail you switched on into your Drive, as "What the app writes" says. Nothing it reads leaves your browser except those files, which go to your own Drive. It never sends, deletes, labels or marks a mail as read, and it reads nothing else.
  • gmail.compose, "Manage drafts and send emails". Asked only while "Create the mail draft" is on for invoices. The app saves one draft per invoice in your Gmail. It never sends mail, although Google's wording for this permission includes sending.
  • script.external_request, which lets a script of yours reach other sites. Asked only while you saved the deployment of a bank sync script in Spending's settings. The app calls that script, which runs in your own Google account, through script.googleapis.com. The script holds your Enable Banking key and calls api.enablebanking.com, which brings the booked rows of the banks you linked into your Spending file. The script stores only one session id per bank and no bank data. Nothing leaves your browser for this while no script is saved.
  • openid and email, "Associate you with your personal info on Google" and "See your primary Google Account email address". Always asked. They tell the app which account signed in, and that is all it reads about your account.

Kuna is not verified by Google. Google shows a warning on its consent screen for the restricted permissions drive.readonly, gmail.readonly and gmail.compose, and the app stays within the 100 users Google allows an unverified app.

A file you open or drop on the page, an .xlsx workbook or a Kuna file, is read in this browser only. So is a receipt photo's QR code. The photo leaves this browser only when you tap Read the lines or switch on Keep the photo on Drive, and it is gone from here when the dialog closes.

What the app writes

In your books, only what you save, and only in kuna-company.json: the records you add, change or delete, and the bank statements a sync files on the ledger. Before each save it reads the file again, and it refuses when a record it means changed since. Each save keeps its revision of the file, and the first save of a day copies the file as it stood into a backups folder beside it. The app keeps the last 30 daily copies and the first of each month.

When you make an invoice, it files the PDF in the invoice folder you picked, making a year or month folder there only when you ask it to, and it never overwrites a file except on a re-export you start.

For Investments, it writes the closes and rates it fetched into kuna-investments-prices.json beside your Investments file, so your other devices read them without asking the vendors again. The file holds no key.

With a file of yours on Drive, each sync writes your keys, for the price vendors and for the assistant, and when you last saved or removed each one, into kuna-secrets.json in Kuna's hidden app folder in your Drive. A key you remove in one browser is removed from the others on their next sync. Drive may keep earlier versions of that file, which only Kuna can read, for about 30 days, as it does for other files.

Remove the Investments file, in the Investments Settings, asks first. For a file on Drive it moves kuna-investments.json and kuna-investments-prices.json to your Drive bin, where you can restore them for 30 days. For a file kept in this browser it deletes the file and its prices here. Your keys stay, in this browser and in Kuna's hidden folder, since every Kuna app uses them, and so do the backups folder and the files you exported.

When you save a receipt, it writes what you confirmed into your Spending file: the date, time and total, the JIR or ZKI, the merchant, its OIB and a buyer's OIB when a read gave them, the lines with their categories, and the model that read them. With Keep the photo on Drive on, it uploads the photo as you took it, which can show a masked card number and a shop's address and, from a phone, can hold the place it was taken, into a Receipts folder beside that file and keeps the file's id with the receipt. Removing the receipt or an Undo leaves that photo on your Drive for you to delete. When the photo you pick holds a GPS position, the page reads it from the file in this browser and your Spending file stores it with the receipt as a latitude and a longitude, whether or not you keep the photo. Kuna sends it to no one, and the photo sent to the assistant leaves it behind. The entry shows those coordinates with a link to openstreetmap.org, which sees them only if you tap it.

While you name the address your bank mails statements from, each sync files the attachments of its new statement mail, the statement PDF and XML, into your statements folder in Drive, in a folder for the year that it makes when it is missing. While you switch on a kind of your accountant's mail, each sync files its new payroll documents, VAT returns or the accountant's invoices into the folder you set for that kind, in a folder for the year, and for the payroll and the invoices one for the month, that it makes when it is missing. Nothing in the app reads those files. Each file it makes carries three labels only this app sees: that the app filed it from mail, the Gmail id of the mail, and the mail's date. The next sync starts a week before the newest file in the folder, whoever filed it. It never trashes, renames or overwrites a file there, and it never changes your mail.

Where your data goes

There is no server. The page talks to Google's own APIs to read and write your books on Drive, read your bank statements and your mail, file the invoices you make and save their drafts, for Investments to the price vendors named below, and for Assets to the Croatian Bureau of Statistics (DZS). No analytics, no tracking, no third-party scripts beyond Google's sign-in and file picker.

For Spending's bank sync, once you saved a script of yours and linked a bank, the booked rows of your linked accounts, with their amounts, dates, descriptions, and the names and IBANs of who paid or was paid, come from your bank through Enable Banking (api.enablebanking.com) to that script in your own Google account, and from there to this page, which saves them in your Spending file. Neither Kuna nor the script keeps a copy elsewhere. Enable Banking's own terms cover what it does, and you approve it at your bank for each bank you link.

For Investments, the page also asks the price vendors named in the Investments Settings for daily closes and exchange rates: Twelve Data, Alpha Vantage and the European Central Bank. It calls them with your own keys, kept in this browser and, with a file of yours on Drive, in Kuna's hidden folder in your Drive, where a vendor needs one. It sends them only the symbols of the instruments and the currencies your records name, plus USD, GBP and CHF, and the day it needs closes from, never an amount or anything else from your books.

The assistant is off for every app until you switch it on in Kuna settings, and it runs only with your own key for Anthropic, OpenAI or OpenRouter, kept like the price vendor keys. Each task says before its first run what it sends. Suggest categories sends the counterparty names, descriptions, amounts, months and accounts of the rows waiting for review, and your categories, with IBANs and OIBs masked and no row that names an employee unless you switch that on. Explain sends a figure's build-up, its amounts and the rules it rests on, and a figure that names employees only once you switch that on. It never sends a ledger row behind a figure or a planned revenue step that names a client or an invoice item, and it never reaches the web. What comes back is only a suggestion on screen, which you save yourself. Read a receipt sends the whole photo, downscaled and without its camera data, which can show a masked card number, a loyalty card and the shop, with your categories and earlier lines at the same shop, and it cannot mask what the photo shows. Testing a key sends nothing from your books. Anthropic deletes what Kuna sends within 30 days, OpenAI keeps it up to 30 days for abuse checks, and neither trains on it. OpenRouter keeps nothing by default, and the model's own provider keeps what its terms say.

For the mixes and the coming income of Investments, it also asks Finnhub, Alpha Vantage and Twelve Data about the instruments you hold: a company's industry and home country from Finnhub, by the instrument's ISIN where it has one and else its symbol, a fund's weights by sector and a listing's dividends from Alpha Vantage, and what a listing is from a search of its own price vendor, by its symbol. When you search in the dialog of a holding, the text you type goes to each of these vendors whose key this browser keeps. It never sends how many units you hold, an amount or anything else from your books. What the vendors answer is kept in the prices file beside your Investments file.

For a flat in your Assets file, the page asks the DZS for its house price index, once each time the page opens. It sends only the query for the index table, never a value, a region or anything else of yours. The answer is kept in memory only, until the page closes.

Where your data is kept

With your books on Google Drive, the file there is the original. The app keeps a copy in this browser's storage so the figures show at once.

The keys you enter for the price vendors and the assistant are kept in this browser's storage. With a file of yours on Drive, each sync also keeps them in Kuna's hidden app folder in your Drive, which Drive does not show, nobody can share and no other app reads, so every browser signed in to your account gets them. They never go into a books file, the prices file or a folder you see. With every file kept in this browser, they stay only in this browser. The assistant's switches and the model you pick stay on this device. A device without keys reads the closes another device fetched. Clearing this site's data in the browser forgets them here. Delete my keys from my Google account, in Kuna settings, deletes the keys file from that folder and leaves this browser's keys, which the next sync saves again while a file of yours is on Drive. Google lets you delete an app's hidden folder yourself, and its help page Use Google Drive apps says where Drive lists the apps you use.

Google's access token is kept only in the open page, so it is gone when you close or reload the page, and it expires about an hour after Google issues it. Nothing in this browser's storage holds a token. The app keeps the email address of the account you signed in with, to skip Google's account chooser, and the names of the permissions Google granted and the books need. Each time the app opens your books on Drive, it asks Google for a new token without showing you a screen. Where Google needs your sign-in or your consent first, the app waits for you to tap. Sign out of Google in Settings forgets the email and the granted permissions and revokes the token.

While you link a bank, this browser keeps the link's one-time state and the code the bank came back with (kuna.bank-link) until the link is written down, what the return came to (kuna.bank-return) until the Bank sync tab shows it, and when it last asked the banks with what they left wanting (kuna.bank-sync-fetched).

With your books kept in this browser, they live in this browser's storage and in the files you export. Clearing this site's data in the browser deletes them, so export to keep a copy, or keep them on Google Drive.

How to revoke access

Remove Kuna at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To remove the copy in this browser, clear the site data for this page in your browser's settings.

Removing Kuna does not end a bank link. To end one, withdraw the consent at your bank or in Enable Banking's Control Panel, and delete the session rows and the key from the script's Script Properties, or delete the script.
