Privacy

Ledgerboard is a web page that works out company finance figures in your browser. Updated 23 October 2026.

What the app reads

Files you choose, and your bank statements. With Google, it asks for the drive.file permission, which covers only the Sheet you pick in Google's file picker and the files the app makes. It reads that Sheet's ledger, categories, accounts, settings and rules tabs.

It also asks for drive.readonly, which lets it read any file in your Drive and change none. It uses it only to find the bank statements in your statements folder that are not on the ledger yet, since drive.file cannot see files another app filed there. Google's wording for this permission covers your whole Drive. The app reads nothing else with it. Each time the app opens, it asks Google for this permission again, and Google shows you a screen only when it needs your consent.

It also asks for openid email, which tells it the email address of the Google account you signed in with. That is all it reads about your account.

When you make an invoice, it lists the folders inside the invoice folder you picked, again only those it made or you picked.

If you turn on "Create the mail draft", it also asks for gmail.compose, which lets it save a draft in your Gmail. It saves one draft per invoice and never sends mail. Google's wording for this permission includes sending, which the app does not do.

In Excel mode it reads only the .xlsx file you open or drop on the page.

What the app writes

Only what you save, and only in the Sheet you picked: the category and flagged_by cells of a ledger row you confirm or recategorise, rows on the rules tab you add or change, and rows on the settings tab you change. Before each write it reads the tab again, and it refuses when the row it means cannot be found exactly once. It never writes any other cell, and it never deletes a row.

When you make an invoice, it files the PDF in the invoice folder you picked, making a year or month folder there only when you ask it to, and it never overwrites a file except on a re-export you start.

Where your data goes

There is no server. The page talks only to Google's own APIs, and only to read and write the Sheet you picked, read your bank statements, file the invoices you make and save their drafts. No analytics, no tracking, no third-party scripts beyond Google's sign-in and file picker.

Where your data is kept

With a Google Sheet, the Sheet stays the original. The app keeps a copy in this browser's storage so the figures show at once.

Google's access token is kept in this browser until it expires, about an hour after you sign in, so a reload or a new tab can sync without asking again. Your email address is stored only to skip Google's account chooser the next time you sign in. Sign out of Google in Settings removes both and revokes the token. The tokens of the Gmail and the drive.readonly permissions are kept only in the open page and are gone when you close or reload it, or sign out.

In Excel mode, the workbook lives in this browser's storage and in the files you export. Clearing this site's data in the browser deletes it, so export to keep a copy.

How to revoke access

Remove Ledgerboard at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To remove the copy in this browser, clear the site data for this page in your browser's settings.
